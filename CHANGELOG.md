# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-10

First public release.

- Import MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG and AAC files by drag and drop or browsing. Originals are never modified.
- Speed from 0.25× to 2.00× in two modes: Classic Slowed, and Time Stretch with Rubber Band.
- Pitch from −12 to +12 semitones, independent of speed.
- Convolution reverb with mix, room size, decay, pre-delay, damping, width and a low cut on the reverb send. Six reverb presets.
- Bass boost, 3-band EQ, high-pass and low-pass filters, output gain, balance and a limiter.
- Ten built-in song presets, plus your own saved presets.
- Waveform with seek, zoom, region selection, trim, loop, fades and exact start/end times.
- A/B compare with the original, and undo/redo for effect changes.
- Stereo level meter with clip indicator, and a spectrum analyser.
- Export to MP3 (up to 320 kbps or V0), WAV and FLAC, as the full song or just the selection, with a progress bar.
- Remembers your last settings, presets and export folder.
